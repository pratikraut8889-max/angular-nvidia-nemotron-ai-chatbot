Which files can I ask questions about?

The current application supports PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, and Markdown files, with a 10 MiB upload limit. Scanned image-only PDFs may not contain extractable text and are not currently OCR-processed.

Does every answer use web search?

No. The application can answer general questions directly and uses web search for detected time-sensitive questions. Web research depends on the server's Tavily configuration and search availability.

How is uploaded document content processed?

The app extracts text, splits it into chunks, and stores the resulting chunks and embeddings for document Q&A. Relevant excerpts may be sent to the configured AI provider to answer questions. Avoid uploading confidential or regulated information unless you are authorized to share it and have reviewed the applicable data-handling terms.

Can AI responses be wrong?

Yes. Models can misunderstand a document or produce incorrect information. Review relevant passages and source links, especially for technical, financial, legal, medical, or business-critical decisions.
