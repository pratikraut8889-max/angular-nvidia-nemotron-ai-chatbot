Uploaded files are processed to support document questions. Only upload material you are authorized to share, and avoid sensitive information unless your organization's policies and the service's data handling terms permit it.

Frequently asked questions

What file types can I upload? +

The current document workflow supports PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, and Markdown files, subject to the application's upload limits.

Does every question use web search? +

No. ConversaNemo.ai can answer general questions directly and use web research when its search logic identifies a need for current information. Search availability depends on service configuration.

Can AI answers contain mistakes? +

Yes. Check source links when available, compare important claims with trusted references, and do not rely on an AI answer as your only source for high-impact decisions.

Are uploaded documents automatically accurate or fully searchable? +

Not always. Extraction quality depends on the file. Scanned PDFs, complex layouts, tables, and unsupported content may need additional processing or manual review.
